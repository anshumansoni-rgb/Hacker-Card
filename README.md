## About
***About Myself***:
This was my fist ever PCB project and I started off with a simple one by making a hacker card (NFC Card), since my friend told me it is the easiest to start off with and TRUST ME, it genuinely was fun designing it, I can't even imagine what it would be like to build it and finally be using it IRL.... what it does is that it acts as a wireless transmitting device which can be used by anyone and coded through their phones or any other device which accepts wireless transmission though antennas on it...

***Why I made this***: To answer the question of why I would make this project is because I wanted a payment gateway/ a card which could redirect people to my payment details by just scanning the QR code on the card itself or tap my card on the back of their phone so that their payment app may open.... To physically implement this, I would need to build this physically and test it out myself to see if it really works or not, if the redirections does not succeed for any reason, I may change the use case of this to something else that I may wish to show, perhaps, it would redirect to my socials where people can follow me (basically this card has no limits to its opportunities, and I am willing to explore all of it ;))

I feel like this is a pretty cool project for a beginner like me, imagine saying someone that "*I BUILT AN NFC CARD MYSELF WHICH CAN TRANSMIT DATA WIRELESS*".... Yes, I know that does sound cool, until you actually know how to build it and then everything just feels easy (it's all about the first step in my opinion)

***Designing of the Card***: Anyways, for it to look even better and match my aesthetics, I added a few patent-ish designs/blueprints of a few tech stuff like a 3d printer, motherboard, a car, and a chip..... This made the project have some depth and overall enhance the expeience of building this from scratch. When you look at the images and designs, it feels like it is so complex but it actually wasn't that hard to actually make these desgins/ inserting them on the PCB.

I had used EasyEDA for designing the PCB layout and also for making the scheatic diagram and a software called Onshape, which is for 3D modelling case designs and both of these softwares support 3D vieweing which basically is an extremely good way to look at what you have made, It genuinely made me astonished that how can something run so well on a browser,, The schematic diagram shows what component of your project connects to which component and also that what parts have we used in the making of the build... and the PCB layout is for showing the PCB manufacturers where they have to place the resistors, capacitors and all the other stuff on the PCB itself, it is like an XRay of your phone or PC's working area and as a first timer, it would most certainly look confusing but once you get a hang of it, you will easily do everything without any struggles. 

***Working Theory***: For anyone thinking, "*Yes, Anshuman, we get it that u built an NFC card all by yourself but what about the power and energy needs of the card, will it be safe or not?*"
To answer that, this card does not need any power supply/battery to operate, it gains power wirelessly and operates entirely through the battery of the phone, hence it is completely safe for keeping near you without any problems... I KNOW ITS COOL RIGHT

***Journey***: What started off with just making a schematic diagram with a bit of help in understanding how the components of this card works, escalated to designing the PCB and then came the case designing part, which in my opinion was the most fun part as I could show my skills in designing a 3D model.... after all this I started making designs and bluepints on the card itself which took quite some time as I had to make it myself.
Overall I would rate this a 11/10 experience and would recommend every single teen to try this out and experience this project at least once


# Well, for the instructions on how you yourself could make this is as follows:
- The first thing is copy or download this repository and include all the files in.
  
- From there you can open the EasyEDA and navigate into schematic and PCB from the .epro src file in the src folder by importing it into EasyEDA pro (I personally would recommend using pro since I had also used it)

- Next step would be that you need to open the PCB editor from in EasyEDA and then change the text according to your needs, or even mine is fine if you like it 

- Then you need to change the QR code (If you are fine with the QR I have, then thats a plus point, since mine directs to my payment page, lol)

- Then if u dont like my patent blueprint theme (though it kinda genuinely looks cool, in my opinion), you can delete the images and then go to file > import > import image  and then import images of your choice (Make sure to import them on the front or back silkscreen not the defult front layer, trust me I have made that mistake)

- That's it, your PCB is done now all u need to do is go onto JLCPCB or PCB Way or any other PCB manufacturer and upload the gerbers (you can get all the fabrication files from easyEDA by going to file > export > gerbers/ pick and place file/ BOM)

- Then all you need to do is select the colour you want the card (From what I like, it should be black, as it just looks good)
  
- select PCBA if you dont want to assemble it yourself (Which you should as it would give you some experience and order the PCB... after waiting a bit you would be having a functional NFC card in your hands!!!
